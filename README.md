## Bank Loan Default Prediction

Our team conducted a study on bank loan default prediction to help financial institutions reduce credit risk and improve loan approval decisions. The analysis was performed on a dataset of 1,500 bank customers with 12 variables, including income, employment duration, and debt-to-income ratio.

After performing descriptive analysis, we applied multiple advanced machine learning techniques, including a Classification Tree (optimized using pruning), Probabilistic Neural Networks (PNN), and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) for prediction. In addition, K-means clustering with silhouette score evaluation was used to segment customers into distinct risk groups.

The Classification Tree identified employment duration, debt-to-income ratio, and credit card debt as the most important predictors of default. The clustering approach revealed a high-risk segment with a 40.31% default rate, mainly consisting of younger customers with lower income and shorter employment and residence history.

Overall, the results show that combining classification and clustering techniques can effectively identify high-risk customers, enabling banks to make more informed decisions and strengthen their credit risk management strategies.
